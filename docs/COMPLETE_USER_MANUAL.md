If you used paper notebooks or spreadsheets:
- You would forget who you called yesterday.

# 2. 🔑 How to Log In & Set Up Your Account

### Step 1: Open the CRM in your Web Browser
Open Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge, and type the website link:
```text
https://vrindaacorp-crm.tech/
```

### Step 2: Sign In
You will see the clean white and green login screen:
1. In the **Email address** box, type your work email (for example: `admin@vrindaacorp.com`).
2. In the **Password** box, type your secret password.
3. Click the bright green **Sign In** button.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you are on your personal computer, your browser will ask *"Save password?"*. Click **Save** so you don't have to type it every time!

### Step 3: Changing Your Password
Want to change your password to something only you know?
1. Look at the bottom-left corner of the screen.
2. Click on **Account** (or your name).
3. Type your current password.
4. Type your new secret password twice.
5. Click **Save New Password**. Done!

### How to Add a Single New Lead Manually

Got a business card at a meeting? Here is how to add them:
1. Go to **Leads**.
2. Click the green button at the top right: **+ Add Lead**.
3. A popup form appears. Fill in:
   - **First Name & Last Name** (e.g., *Rajesh Kumar*)
   - **Company Name** (e.g., *DLF CyberPark Offices*)
   - **Email Address** (e.g., *rajesh@cyberpark.com*)
   - **Phone Number** (e.g., *+91 98765 43210*)
   - **Sector / Industry** (choose *Corporate*)
   - **City / Region** (e.g., *Gurgaon, NCR*)
4. Click **Create Lead**.
5. The CRM immediately checks if the email syntax is correct and adds them to your database!

---

### Understanding the Traffic Light Colors: Email Validation

Every lead has a colored badge next to their email:

| Badge | Color | What It Means | Can We Email Them? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VALID** | 🟢 Green | The email domain exists, mail server accepted it, 100% safe! | ✅ Yes, send immediately! |
| **RISKY** | 🟡 Yellow | Catch-all email domain or unverifiable inbox. | ⚠️ Be cautious. |
| **INVALID** | 🔴 Red | Fake email, typo, or deleted account. | 🚫 **Never email!** CRM blocks it to protect your sender reputation. |
| **SUPPRESSED**| ⛔ Black | The person asked to unsubscribe or bounced. | 🚫 Permanently blocked from all emails. |

# 7. 📥 How to Import Leads from an Excel or CSV File

If you have a spreadsheet with hundreds of company contacts, don't type them one by one! Use the **Import** tool.

### Step 1: Prepare Your File
Make sure your file is saved as `.csv` or `.xlsx`. It should have column headers at the top, like:
`First Name`, `Last Name`, `Company`, `Email`, `Sector`, `City`, `Phone`.

### Step 2: Upload
1. Click **Import** in the sidebar.
2. Drag and drop your file into the dashed box, or click **Browse Files** to pick it from your computer.
3. Click **Upload & Preview**.

### Step 3: Column Matching (Connect the Dots)
The CRM is smart, but it asks you to confirm which column is which:
- Tell it: *"My 'Org Name' column means 'Company'"*.
- Tell it: *"My 'Work Email' column means 'Email'"*.

### Step 4: Run the Import Robot
Click **Start Import**. The CRM will:
- Check for duplicate emails (it will never import the same person twice!).
- Normalize messy city and sector names automatically.
- Check against the suppression list.
- Show you a green success badge: *"Successfully imported 450 new leads!"*

# 9. 🚀 Email Campaigns & Automated Sequences (The Outreach Engine)

This is the most powerful part of the CRM. Instead of sitting and typing 500 emails by hand, you configure an automated sequence that drips emails out over several days.

### How to Create a New Campaign:
1. Click **Campaigns** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Campaign**.
3. Give it a name (e.g., *"Healthcare Hospitals Q4 Outreach"*).
4. Click **Create**.

---

### Step 1: Target Segment (Who Gets the Email?)
On the campaign screen, choose your target audience:
- **Sector**: Pick an industry (like *Healthcare*) or leave as *Any sector*.
- **Geography**: Pick a region (like *NCR*) or leave as *Any geography*.
- **Validation**: Keep set to *Valid only* (safe sending).
- Click **Preview count** to see how many leads match (e.g., *"142 leads match"*).
- Click **Save segment**.

---

### Step 2: Sequence Steps (The Zero-Blocker Magic!)

> 🌟 **NEW FEATURE:** You NEVER have to manually look for email templates anymore!

In the **Sequence steps** card:
- If your campaign is set to an industry (like *Healthcare*), the CRM **automatically selects the Healthcare dietary & nutrition email template!**
- If your campaign has **no industry specified** (or *Any sector*), the CRM **calls Ollama AI deployed on your VPS server to write a brand new, highly professional B2B template on the spot!**

#### Adding Steps:
- **Step 1 (First Outreach)**: Delay is `0 days` (sent immediately).
- **Step 2 (Gentle Follow-up)**: Delay is `3 days` (automatically sent 3 days later only if they haven't replied!).
- **Step 3 (Final Check-in)**: Delay is `6 days`.

#### Two Easy Ways to Add Steps:
1. Click the green button **⚡ Auto-Add with AI**: The system will automatically craft the step and template for you!
2. Or click **Add step** with the dropdown left on *"✨ Auto-select by industry or generate with Ollama AI"*.

#### Previewing Any Step:
Want to see what Step 1 looks like before sending?  
Click the grey **Preview** button on any step row. A popup will show you the exact email with real prospect names filled in!

# 11. 📝 Email Templates & Magic Merge Tags

Click on **Templates** in the sidebar to view or edit email templates.

### What are Magic Merge Tags?
When you write an email, you don't want to type *"Dear Customer"*. You want to say *"Dear Rahul"* and mention *"Apex Towers"*.

Instead of typing names manually, use **double curly braces**:

| Merge Tag | What the CRM Replaces It With | Example in Real Email |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `{{firstName}}` | The person's first name | *"Hi Vikram,"* |
| `{{company}}` | Their company name | *"Facility operations at Max Healthcare"* |
| `{{city}}` | Their city | *"Our teams across Noida & Greater Noida"* |
| `{{industryHook}}` | Customized industry value pitch | *"HACCP-certified hospital dietary catering"* |
| `{{unsubscribe}}` | A safe opt-out link | Automatically creates a 1-click unsubscribe link |

### A/B Testing Subject Lines:
Every template allows:
- **Subject A**: *"Streamlining facility operations at {{company}}"*
- **Subject B**: *"Quick question regarding cafeteria management for {{company}}"*

The CRM will send Subject A to half of your list, and Subject B to the other half. It tracks which one gets more opens so you know what works best!

### 1. ☀️ 09:00 AM Morning Game Plan
Every morning at 09:00 AM (IST), the AI agent sends a game plan to your WhatsApp:
- Planned outreach volume for today
- Target business sectors (e.g., Healthcare, Manufacturing, Corporate)
- Daily pacing cap (emails/day)
- Unassigned verified lead pool remaining

### 2. ⚡ Inbound Client Revert Handling (Zero Delay)
When a prospective client replies to any outreach email:
1. The IMAP listener picks up their email within 30 seconds.
2. The local Ollama AI reads their message, understands their requirements, and generates a personalized B2B proposal draft.
3. The draft is sent straight to your WhatsApp with the prospect's details and an excerpt of what they said.
4. **How you respond on WhatsApp:**
   - Reply **"YES"** or **"SEND"** ➔ The agent instantly dispatches the email to the client via Google SMTP.
   - Reply with revision instructions (e.g., *"Make it shorter and offer a meeting this Thursday at 3 PM"*) ➔ The AI regenerates the draft and sends Version 2 for your confirmation.

- **Deliverability & Bounce Rate**: Confirmation of 100% clean inboxing (<2% bounce rate)
- **Emails Opened Today & Unique Readers**: Engagement counts and overall open rate %
- **Overall Response Rate**: Reply percentage across campaigns
- **High-Intent Repeat Openers**: A prioritized list of prospects who opened your email **more than once** today (with company name, contact, and number of opens) — prime targets for a direct phone call!

### 4. 📈 Bi-Weekly Domain Scaling & Warmup Strategy (Every 14 Days)
To protect `vrindaacorp.com`'s mailbox reputation and prevent blacklisting:
- Every 14 days, the AI analyzes rolling deliverability, bounce rates, and open rates.
- It recommends safe warmup scaling tiers:
  - *Tier 1 (Days 1–14)*: 25–35 emails/day
  - *Tier 2 (Days 15–28)*: 50–75 emails/day
  - *Tier 3 (Days 29–42)*: 100–125 emails/day
  - *Tier 4 (Scale)*: 150+ emails/day
- It sends the strategy briefing to your WhatsApp with an explicit button / message:
  - Simply reply **"APPROVE STRATEGY"** to safely update the daily quota in 1 tap!

# 16. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

### Q1: I clicked "Add Step", but didn't pick a template. Will it break?
**No!** The CRM now has automatic intelligence. If you leave the template on *"✨ Auto-select by industry or generate with Ollama AI"*, it will automatically pick the best template for your industry, or create a brand new one using Ollama AI!

### Q2: Why did an email not send to a specific lead?
Check the lead's validation badge:
- If their email is marked **INVALID** (red) or **SUPPRESSED** (black), the CRM deliberately stops the email to protect your email domain from being marked as spam.

### Q3: How do I stop a campaign if I made a mistake?
Go to **Campaigns** → Click your campaign → Click the grey **Pause** button. All upcoming scheduled emails will immediately stop sending.

### Q4: Can I test an email on myself first?
**Yes!** Go to **Leads** → Add a lead with your personal email address → Go to your campaign → Enroll that lead and click **Trigger Outreach Now** with limit set to `1`. Check your personal inbox!

### Q5: What happens if a lead replies with "Please remove me"?
When a lead replies or clicks the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email, the CRM automatically marks them **Suppressed**. They will never receive another automated email from any campaign.

# 17. 📖 Glossary: CRM Terms Explained in Plain English

| Term | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lead** | A potential customer or company that might buy our services. |
| **Prospect** | Another word for a lead who has shown some interest. |
| **Drip Campaign** | Sending 2 or 3 emails spaced a few days apart like a slow dripping tap. |
| **Sequence Step** | One specific email in a multi-email campaign (e.g. Step 1, Step 2). |
| **Merge Tag** | Fill-in-the-blank codes like `{{firstName}}` that fill in real names. |
| **Validation** | Checking if an email address is real before sending an email to it. |
| **Bounce** | When an email cannot be delivered because the address does not exist. |
| **Suppression** | A "do not call / do not email" list that protects people who unsubscribed. |
